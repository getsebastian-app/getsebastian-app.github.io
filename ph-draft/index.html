6. Launch Day Checklist

Pre-Launch (1-3 weeks before)

  • Set up PH teaser page, collect followers
  • Prepare all visuals and upload to PH draft
  • Record demo video (30-45 sec)
  • Write and finalize maker's comment
  • Build launch supporter list (friends, community, OpenClaw users, Twitter)
  • Draft tweets, Telegram posts, LinkedIn posts
  • Prepare "launch kit" link with assets for supporters
  • Consider finding a PH Hunter with large following
  • Complete PH maker profiles (real photos)

Launch Day (PH resets 00:01 PT / 08:01 UTC)

07:30 UTC — Final check: visuals uploaded, description finalized, comment ready

08:01 UTC — Launch live! Post maker's first comment immediately. Upvote own product.

08:05-08:30 — Personal messages to supporter list + Twitter + Telegram + LinkedIn

09:00-12:00 — ⚡ CRITICAL 4 HOURS — respond to EVERY comment within 15 min. Monitor ranking. Second wave outreach if needed.

12:00-18:00 — Continue responding. Share progress on Twitter. Engage with other launches.

18:00-24:00 — Third wave (US West Coast). "Thank you" update on Twitter.

Post-Launch (2-3 days)

  • Continue engaging with PH comments
  • Write "lessons learned" post if #1
  • Follow up with everyone who supported
⚠️ DO NOT: Ask for upvotes in exchange for anything · Use fake accounts · Send mass generic DMs · Post in unrelated communities · Stop engaging after first hours
